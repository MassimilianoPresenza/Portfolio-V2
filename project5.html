About the project

As part of the ongoing CMD project The Memory of the Mirror, I participated in the first development phase (Fase A) of an interactive media art installation. This phase focused on experimenting with Visual input to explore how viewers could directly influence digital artworks through movement & presence.

During Fase A, I tested early design and interaction concepts in a live setting during Museumnacht Maastricht at the Natuurhistorisch Museum. The goal was to see if the project was on track or required further fine-tuning.

This testing moment helped validate the feasibility of the concept and gave valuable insights into real user behavior. It also introduced me to rapid prototyping in a live art context, collaborative exhibition preparation, and the creative potential of technology in interactive design.
