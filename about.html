Who I am

I'm the kind of person who watches Interstellar and thinks "I want to build a black hole". Then I do, in p5.js, with live music.

I'm a curious, out-of-the-box thinker who loves exploring new concepts, and I follow gaming, tech and digital art, from CS2 market dynamics to Tech. My motto: with the right mindset, anything is possible.

Detail-oriented and collaborative. I start with research, brainstorming and experimenting, then refine the result through teamwork and feedback.
